# About the Courses

CPT363 runs as three sections on this site, each organised its own way. This page holds what they share – replace these examples with your own.

## The Sections

| Section | Organised by | Taught by |
|---|---|---|
| [CPT363-1](/cpt363-basic) | Modules | Some Name |
| [CPT363-2](/cpt363-intermediate) | Modules | Some Name |
| [CPT363-3](/cpt363-advanced) | Weekly units | Some Name & Another Name |

All three cover the same topics and assignments, so you can follow along in any section.

## Important Dates

- **May 9** – classes start (Week 1)
- **Jul 17** – Project User Research Report due
- **Jul 31** – Project Usability Tested Mockups due
- **Aug 1** – last class (Week 13)
- **Aug 3** – Course Reflection Log due

## Shared Policies

- **Late work** – let your instructor know before the due date if you need more time
- **Participation** – take part in the class discussions each week, in your own section
- **Academic integrity** – your work is your own; cite the ideas and work of others

## Getting Help

Ask your instructor first – their contact details and office hours are in each course's sidebar. For technology help, accessibility services or the library, contact your institution's student services.
